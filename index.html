Intro

Welcome to Stressly’s “Balancing the Student Life” prototype. This interactive visualization explores how students manage the everyday pressures of academics, work, routines, and personal wellbeing. Each section highlights real patterns and experiences, showing how different activities, responsibilities, and stressors shape a student’s day-to-day life. Use the controls to navigate, explore data, and gain insight into how balance and imbalance emerges in the student lifestyle. From the sleep they miss to the coping mechanisms they grow, explore the data to see how pressure shapes the student experience.

Visualizations Overview

The next two visualizations explore student stress and well-being. The Classroom of Stress shows how stress levels vary by year, major, and gender, while the Stick Figure Map with RadarMaps lets you examine individual students’ wellness profiles across multiple dimensions. Together, they highlight both overall patterns and personal variation in student experiences.

The Classroom of Stress

Step into a virtual lecture hall where every student tells a story. This visualization maps the entire student body, revealing the hidden prevalence of stress across different demographics.

ℹ️ How to Explore

  • Observe: Each figure represents a real student.
  • Color Code: Red = High Stress, Orange = Moderate, Blue = Low.
  • Interact: Hover over any student to reveal their specific major, year, and stress score.
  • Filter: Use the menu (top-left) to isolate specific groups like "Freshmen" or "CS Majors".

Wellness Profiles

Stress rarely comes alone. This map explores the multidimensional nature of student wellness, correlating stress with sleep, exercise, anxiety, and depression.

ℹ️ How to Analyze

  • Modes: Use the toolbar (top-right of chart) to sort students by Sleep, Exercise, or Severity.
  • Deep Dive: Hover over any stick figure to expand a detailed Radar Chart showing their balance of 5 key wellness metrics.
  • Pattern Finding: Look for clusters of similar colors to identify high-risk groups.

The Sleep-Stress Connection

"I'll sleep when I'm done." — A common mantra, but at what cost?

The Sleep Orbit

Visualize the rhythm of rest. This interactive orbit demonstrates how sleep timing and duration directly impact academic pressure and mental health.

ℹ️ How to Simulate

  • Adjust: Drag the sliders to change Bed Time, Sleep Duration, and Wake Up Time.
  • Visualize: The blue arc represents the sleep window on a 24-hour clock.
  • Predict: Watch the statistics update to see the predicted stress level for a student with that schedule.

The Emotional Triangle

Stress, Anxiety, and Depression often form a triad.
Understanding how they interact is key to finding balance.

Emotion Constellation

This ternary plot reveals the delicate balance between Stress, Anxiety, and Depression. Each point is a student, positioned by the relative intensity of these three emotions. Corners represent extreme dominance of one emotion, while the center represents a mix.

ℹ️ How to Interpret

  • Position: Points near a corner have high levels of that specific emotion. Center points are balanced.
  • Select: Drag a box (Brush) or hold Shift to Lasso a specific group of students.
  • Tools: Toggle the Heatmap to see density clusters.

The Coping Garden

A metaphorical garden where every flower is a coping strategy. Some are tall and strong (effective), others are small but numerous (popular).

ℹ️ How to Harvest

  • Metaphor:
    🌸 Flower Type = Strategy
    📏 Height = Effectiveness (Taller is better)
    📐 Size = Popularity.
  • Explore: Hover over flowers to see the strategy name and stats.
  • Connect: Click a flower to highlight students in the Triangle who use that strategy.
