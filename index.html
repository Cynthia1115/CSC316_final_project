Intro

Welcome to Stressly’s “Balancing the Student Life” prototype. This interactive visualization explores how students manage the everyday pressures of academics, work, routines, and personal wellbeing. Each section highlights real patterns and experiences, showing how different activities, responsibilities, and stressors shape a student’s day-to-day life. Use the controls to navigate, explore data, and gain insight into how balance and imbalance emerges in the student lifestyle.

The next two visualizations explore student stress and well-being. The Classroom of Stress shows how stress levels vary by year, major, and gender, while the Stick Figure Map with RadarMaps lets you examine individual students’ wellness profiles across multiple dimensions. Together, they highlight both overall patterns and personal variation in student experiences.

Classroom of Stress

Pictograph of students colored by stress category.

Stick Figure Map with Spiderchart

Hover a figure → see student stress profile.

The Sleep Orbit Map lets you explore how sleep habits relate to stress. By adjusting the sliders for time in bed, time asleep, and wake-up time, you can see how different sleep patterns affect predicted stress levels.

Sleep Orbit Map (Novel)

Sections are awake time, time to fall asleep, sleep, and wake up.

The Emotion Constellation Triangle shows how students feel in terms of stress, anxiety, and depression. Each dot represents a student, with its position and color showing how balanced or severe their emotional state is. This helps users see patterns and groups of students with similar emotional experiences.

Emotion Constellation Triangle

Each dot positions a student by normalized Stress, Anxiety, and Depression.

The Coping Strategy Garden shows how different coping behaviors relate to student stress.

Coping Strategy Garden

Each flower = a coping strategy. Height = stress reduction.
